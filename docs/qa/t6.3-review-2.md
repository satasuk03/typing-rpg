**Source.** Every capture was taken on real Metal at 1280×720 (`playwright.metal.config.ts`, port 5311) from `main` at 43a8a88. Nothing from the W-leftover fix branch is in it, so this is the "before" baseline for the Golem wash, the fireball glare and the parry/BREAK work.

- **Console errors:** 0 in every run.
- **How the numbers were measured.** All figures come from the PNGs.
  - Luma is Rec. 709. "Clip" means luma ≥ 0.94; "white" means every channel ≥ 0.9.
  - The "96 px window" figure is the largest share of clipped pixels in any 96×96 window of the play area (y 130–600).
  - Boss boxes come from the stage projector's head and feet anchors.

## 2. Known leftovers: confirmed or refuted

All three are **confirmed**, with numbers below.

1. **L10 boss intro wash: confirmed, and moderate.** The HUD is now hidden [01].
   - Golem body box at intro progress 0.05 / 0.2 / 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8 / 0.95: mean luma 0.32 / 0.39 / **0.52** / 0.45 / 0.40 / 0.39 / 0.42; saturation 0.54 → **0.28** → 0.38.
   - The wash peaks at about 0.35–0.45. At that point 21% of the body is above 0.8 luma and there are about 11% "mote" pixels (white-cyan pixel sparks) [02].
   - The standard still (p 0.45, tighter body box) has mean luma 0.67, p95 0.985 and 6% clipped [01].
   - **Cause:** `BossFx.ignite()`:
     - `kit.flash(…, 1.5, 8, 1.0)` is placed in front of the golem (z + 1.5), lighting the pale stone face-on for 1 s.
     - 60 pixel sparks of [0.8, 2.8, 3.2] live 1.2 s over the body.
     - `postFlash` adds a 220 ms flash on top.
2. **Fireball and auto-slash white glare in the forest: confirmed.**
   - `metal-real-skillcast` [07]: the 220×220 blob beside the slime is **59.8% clipped, 48.7% pure white**, mean luma 0.87. The pre-fix still was 62.3%, so it is unchanged. The weapon-tip flash is 44.6% clipped.
   - In the real 60 WPM runs, a ≥ 60%-clipped 96 px window appears in **4/25 L05 combat frames and 3/23 L01 frames**. The worst windows are 96–100% clipped ([08]: 95.7% at the auto-slash; [09]: 71.7% at the parry star).
   - At 110 WPM: 5/16 frames. The same effect appears on the chest drop: 52% white in a 180×210 box [25].
   - Crit on a small enemy: the bat box is 89% white [crit still, not committed].
   - **Causes:**
     - Crit/strong auto-attack: `AttackFx` star 3.4 u HDR [3, 2.4, 1] plus `flash(…, 3, 6, 0.22)`.
     - Fireball: `SkillFx.explode`, with star 4.5 u, glow 1.5→5 u, `flash 3.2 / r7`, and `postFlash 0.08`.
     - The forest's base luma (0.55–0.7) leaves no headroom, and none of these scale with biome.
3. **Parry hex shards and BREAK ring barely visible: confirmed on small enemies.** The opposite problem appears on the boss (P1-1).
   - Small-enemy Break [12]: the "ring" reads as a soft blue/pink halo with no line. The 14 hex shards and 34 pixel shards read as round, blurred "snowballs" 6–12 px wide. The box is only 9.9% clipped and has no edge structure.
   - Parry [10][11]: the 6 shards are pale squares at about α 0.3 that read as dust. In the forest the box is only 4.5% clipped and the shards are the same colour as the bokeh.
   - **Cause:** the particles sit at the enemy or contact depth, inside the tilt-shift/DOF blur, so the hex silhouette is lost. Their HDR colours ([0.35, 1.25, 1.9], [0.45, 0.95, 1.7]) are too close to the bright bokeh behind them.

## 3. New findings below the bar

- **N1. Orphaned guard barrier (bug).**
  - **What happens:** a typed guard word snaps `GuardBarrier` to M_HELD ("held until impact"). When the enemy's attack is then absorbed by an Aegis charge (`EnemyAttack.outcome === "barrier"`), nothing fades the barrier: `TypingWorldFx` only fades on `outcome === "hit"`.
  - **What you see:** a 2.2 u glass sphere stays ~110 px in front of the hero through rewards, the whole walk and into the next encounter (L05 at 110 WPM, frames 13 → 29, more than 14 s) [13][14].
  - **How often:** it will happen in any run that uses Aegis together with guards, which is the starter kit.
- **N2. Boss Break = white dome.**
  - Every `Break` on the golem (6 in 54 s of the fight) turns the golem into a white sphere about 470×440 px for roughly 0.5–1 s: the box is **55.6% clipped and 61.3% white** [03].
  - For the rest of the break window the golem stays a pale ghost (body mean luma 0.65–0.76, saturation 0.22–0.30) [04].
  - **Causes:**
    - `AttackFx.brk` sizes the hex flash from the enemy's height (`w = max(2.4, height × 1.15)`), so the "small hex flash inside the body" becomes a 4.6×6.6 u disc on the golem. It is stacked with a 5.2 u ring, a 2.4 u star, `flash(1.4, r6)`, `postFlash` and rim [2, 3, 4].
    - `stage.ts` gives the "broken" pose a constant 0.28–0.38 blue-white flash for the whole break, which bypasses the 0.5 boss cap logic. That is fine on dark sprites and washes out the pale golem.
- **N3. Boss phase change is white.** `BossFx.bossPhase` has a star of 7 u at HDR [3, 2.8, 2.4] and `flash(… 6, 9, 0.7)`, which is intensity 6. Fight frame 10 (BossPhaseChanged + Break) has the golem box **66% clipped**.
- **N4. Two translucent spheres around the hero.** With Aegis plus a held guard barrier, the hero sits behind two overlapping glass spheres (hex lattice and gold or azure), with a third floor ring under them [13]. That is clutter, and the hero's saturation drops to 0.38 in the forest. The barrier should sit in front (contact side) and be smaller when Aegis is up.
- **N5. Guard label and panel text are hit by HUD debris.** This is the HUD remainder of #19 and #25: sparks and shards have no avoid rects for the guard label or HERO_PANEL text.
- **N6. Next-letter graze.** In [08] ("floor"), the ATB streak head and a white glint touch the top of the next letter "l". It is 2 px from the stroke, so R3's +4 px inflation is not met on that frame, although the glyph stays legible. Likely cause: the streak-head sprite radius is not included in the clip-out test.
- **N7. Tier 3–4 is almost never reached in real play.**
  - Across 5 bot runs (60–110 WPM, 89–96% accuracy, about 3 min of combat), the key streak peaked at **tier 3 (74)**, and **tier 4 never happened**. At 95% per-key accuracy, P(100-key streak) ≈ 0.6%.
  - The prismatic T4 look, which is the design target per R9, is effectively unreachable for the learners the game is for.
  - This is not an art fix; the PO should decide on it (§6).

## 4. NEW prioritised fix list

Owner tracks: **W** = world/WebGL VFX (`src/render/**`, `src/level/eventBindings.ts`); **H** = HUD canvas (`src/hud/**`). `stage.ts` (the broken-pose tint) is world presentation, so it goes to W, and W should flag it, because `src/level/stage.ts` is outside the W path list.

### P1: blocks PO sign-off

**P1-1. Boss Break white dome + broken ghost** (W). Files: `render/vfx/combat/AttackFx.ts` (`brk`), `level/stage.ts` (broken pose).
- Clamp `w = min(3.0, max(2.4, height × 0.6))` for bosses, and put the hex disc behind the body (z − 0.4).
- Boss values: hex α 0.5 → 0.3; star 2.4 → 1.6 u; flash 1.4 → 0.7; no postFlash; rim [2, 3, 4] → [0.9, 1.3, 1.8].
- Broken pose on bosses: flash 0.28–0.38 → **0.10–0.14**, colour [0.45, 0.75, 1] → [0.35, 0.55, 1.0].
- Make the BREAK legible through **shape**: a crisp ring at ≥ 3 px line width plus 10–14 large hex shards (size ≥ 0.35 u, so ≥ 16 px on screen).
- **Targets:** golem body box ≤ 10% clipped and mean luma ≤ 0.6 on every frame of a Break; saturation ≥ 0.3 during break. Sample at least 10 Breaks in the L10 bot run and report the maximum.

**P1-2. Orphaned guard barrier** (W). File: `render/vfx/TypingWorldFx.ts` (`EnemyAttack` case), plus a fail-safe in `render/vfx/GuardBarrier.ts`.
- `fade(200)` when the outcome is `"hit"` **or `"barrier"`**.
- Also fade on `EncounterCleared`, `WalkStarted` and `HeroDowned`.
- M_HELD auto-fades after 2.5 s with no impact.
- **Target:** zero barrier quads with intensity > 0 during the rewards and walk phases. Add a unit test that drives GuardWordTyped → EnemyAttack{barrier} and checks the intensity reaches 0 within 300 ms, plus an L05 at 110 WPM bot assertion.

**P1-3. Forest glare from crit/auto-slash/fireball/chest** (W). Files: `AttackFx.ts` (crit/strong slash star and flash), `SkillFx.ts` (`explode`, `castFlash`), `RewardFx.ts` (chest).
- Scale the additive star, glow and light intensity by `lerp(0.45, 1, caveK)`.
- Cap the star core HDR at 1.6 in the forest.
- Fireball: glow 1.5→5 u becomes 1.2→3.2 u; flash 3.2 → 1.6; postFlash 0.08 → 0.04.
- Give the fireball impact a hot orange core [2.0, 0.9, 0.25] instead of the white star centre.
- **Targets:** in the L05/L01 60 WPM bot runs, no 96 px window is more than 35% clipped (it is 72–100% today), and the blob box beside the target is ≤ 15% clipped and ≤ 8% pure white (59.8% / 48.7% today).

**P1-4. Golem intro wash** (W). File: `render/vfx/combat/BossFx.ts` (`ignite`).
- Light: `flash(…, 1.5, 8, 1.0)` → `(…, 0.6, 4, 0.6)`, moved behind the golem (z − 0.6) so it rims the stone instead of lighting it face-on.
- Pixel sparks: 60 → 24, colour [0.5, 1.4, 1.6], size 0.06, and spawn them at the feet and rune ring rather than across the torso.
- Keep the star, which is already behind the head.
- **Targets:** at intro progress 0.35–0.5, golem body box mean luma ≤ 0.45, saturation ≥ 0.4, ≥ 0.8-luma share ≤ 8%, mote pixels ≤ 4% (today 0.52–0.67 / 0.26–0.28 / 21% / 11%).

**P1-5. Parry and small-enemy BREAK need shape, not glow** (W). Files: `render/vfx/GuardBarrier.ts` (parry), `AttackFx.ts` (`brk` for non-boss), and the particle depth or DOF.
- Spawn the shards on the focus plane (the hero and enemy z the DOF focuses on), or render the shard pool after DOF.
- Shards: 6 → 8, size 0.26 → 0.34 u (≥ 14 px), life 0.55 → 0.7 s. Use a dark-rimmed hex sprite with the core [0.35, 1.25, 1.9] at α 1.0 and a 1 px #0b2a4a edge, so they read on bright bokeh.
- Parry core: #7fe8ff with intensity ≤ 1.0, so the clip share in the contact box is ≤ 3%.
- Break ring: line 3 px, colour [0.6, 1.2, 2.0], radius to 2.2 u, 0.35 s.
- **Targets:** in `metal-parry-forest` and `metal-break`, at least 4 shards are individually countable at 100% zoom. The ring is a continuous arc with ≥ 0.25 luma contrast against a 16 px surround.

## 5. "Juicy" readiness verdict

**Verdict: not yet. It is close, and the remaining gap is concrete.**

**What is ready:**
- The word-complete pipeline is spectacular in both biomes:
  - plate shatter;
  - letter fragments that fly as real glyphs into the blade ([24]: "b-e-a-r" trailing into the sword);
  - PERFECT rings and rays [23];
  - BREAK pops [17];
  - sentence bolts [19];
  - the finisher cinematic [20].
- Tier 3–4 auras look great: forest violet [21], cave magenta [22].
- The boss doom sentence is the best frame in the game [06].
- Readability holds. In about 160 real-run frames, no VFX covered a word plate. Letter-level issues were limited to one 2 px graze (N6) and the guard-timer label (P2-2).

**What would let the PO down:**
1. The boss Break white dome. It happens every ~9 s of the boss fight and erases the golem (P1-1, N2).
2. White glare blobs in the forest from fireball, crit and chest. About 1 in 6 combat frames has one (P1-3).
3. The orphaned glass sphere floating in front of the hero during walks (P1-2). It looks broken.
4. Parry and Break on small enemies feel soft, with no crunchy shards (P1-5).
5. Per-keystroke feedback at tier 0–1 is competent but quiet on the bright forest. The spectacular per-key look (tiers 3–4) needs 50–100-key streaks, which a typical learner rarely or never reaches (N7).

**Recommendation.** Do P1-1…P1-5 plus P2-2 and P2-7, then show the PO. Separately, ask the PO about N7. Options are softer tier thresholds (T3 ≥ 30, T4 ≥ 60) or not fully resetting the streak tier on one typo (drop one tier). That is a sim/design change, outside W/H.
